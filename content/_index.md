# About Me


Hi! I’m a second-year [ELLIS](https://ellis.eu/phd-postdoc) Ph.D. student in computer science at [Mila](https://mila.quebec/) and [Université de Montréal](https://diro.umontreal.ca/). I’m fortunate to be advised by the fantastic duo of [Prof. Simon Lacoste-Julien](https://www.iro.umontreal.ca/~slacoste/) and [Prof. Moritz Hardt](https://www.mrtz.org/) (at the [Max Planck Institute for Intelligent Systems](https://is.mpg.de/) in Tübingen, Germany).

I’m broadly interested in understanding the societal impacts of AI from a theoretical perspective, using tools from learning theory, optimization, game theory, and mechanism design. These days, I’m working closely with Prof. Gauthier Gidel on designing ranking algorithms for benchmarking and LLM evaluation based on pairwise preference evaluations. A key goal of this work is to make these rankings robust to strategic gaming, drawing on ideas from social choice theory.

Before starting my Ph.D., I was a Pre-Doctoral Researcher at Google DeepMind India, where I worked with [Dr. Rishi Saket](https://research.google/people/107857/) and [Dr. Aravindan Raghuveer](https://research.google/people/107631/).
